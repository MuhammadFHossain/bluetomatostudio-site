ReadyOn this phone

Talk in any app, and brevva keys types it for you.

brevva keys is a keyboard for iPhone with dictation built in. Tap the mic key and the keys turn into a small display that listens. Your words appear as you speak. Tap Add and they drop into the message, email or note you were writing.

  • US English
  • No account

How it works

Tap the mic, talk, tap Add.

  1. Tap the mic

    The mic on the brevva keys keyboard, in amber. The keys turn into a small display that listens.

  2. Talk

    Your words appear as you speak. It listens for up to three minutes at a time.

  3. Tap Add

    The words go in where you were typing, already tidied. You never leave the app you are in.

You can also start from the Action Button, or by saying “Dictate with brevva keys” to Siri. The globe and microphone in the strip below the keys belong to iOS: that microphone starts Apple’s own dictation.

Which version

Listening inside the keyboard is new in version 2.1. In version 2.0 the mic key opens the brevva keys app to listen, and your words are waiting when you go back. The version number is at the bottom of the app’s Settings.

The tidy

It takes out the ums and keeps your correction.

Every dictation goes through brevva’s own rules on the phone before it is typed. Filler words like “um” come out and punctuation goes in. Change your mind halfway (“at two, actually three”) and only the correction stays.

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple’s on-device model then gives the words a second, careful tidy a moment after they go in. Say a list and it comes out as a list. If you have already typed on, it leaves your words as they are.

Typing

When you would rather type, it feels like the keyboard you know.

The same layout, the same key sizes and the same feel as the keyboard that came with your iPhone. Autocorrect is built to keep up with fast thumbs.

Your words

It learns your words, and spells your phrases your way.

Names, places and the words you keep typing go into your own dictionary on the iPhone, so autocorrect stops changing them. Every word it has learned is listed in the app, and you can delete any of them.

Where your words go

What happens to what you say and type.

Speech, typing and tidying all happen on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server of ours. Here it is item by item.

Everything brevva keys handles, where it is handled, and whether it leaves the iPhone.
WhatWhere it is handledLeaves the iPhone
Keys you tap or swipeThe keyboard, on the iPhone.No
Your voice, in the keyboardApple’s on-device dictation model. The sound is not saved.No

The only downloads

  • The first time you open the app, it downloads its voice files (about 600 MB) from Hugging Face, which hosts the speech model. Like any download, that shows your IP address to Hugging Face. Nothing you say or type is part of it.
  • If Apple’s US English dictation model is not on your iPhone yet, the app asks iOS to fetch it from Apple.
  • Nothing is uploaded.

Why it asks for Full Access

iOS warns that a keyboard with Full Access could send what you type over the network. brevva keys uses Full Access for three things on the phone: the microphone, the folder it shares with its app, and the clipboard, for your recent copies. The keyboard makes no network connections of its own.

Typing works with Full Access off. Dictation, learning your words and recent copies need it on.

Check it yourself

  • Airplane modeOnce the voice files are in, turn on Airplane Mode and dictate. Nothing it does needs a server.
  • App Store labelThe listing’s App Privacy section says Data Not Collected. The privacy policy spells out what is kept on the phone.
  • Privacy reportiOS lists every domain an app contacts: Settings › Privacy & Security › App Privacy Report.

Before you install

Who it suits, and who should wait.

YesIt suits you if

  • You write in English on an iPhone with iOS 26.2 or later.
  • You want dictation that tidies itself without a server.
  • Autocorrect keeps changing names or phrases you meant.
  • You would like to start talking from the Action Button or Siri.

Not yetWait if

  • You type mostly in another language. Typing and dictation are US English only for now.
  • You want it on iPad. It is an iPhone keyboard.
  • You want your dictionary on more than one device. It stays on the iPhone it learned on.
  • You cannot spare about 600 MB, or a Wi-Fi connection, the first time you open it.
  • You will not turn on Full Access. Typing works without it; dictation does not.

The second tidy needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, the rules still tidy every dictation.

After you install

Setting it up.

  1. Open brevva keys

    It downloads its voice files once (about 600 MB) and asks to use the microphone and speech recognition. Stay on Wi-Fi with the app open until it says ready.

  2. Add the keyboard

    Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard, then pick brevva keys.

  3. Allow Full Access

    Tap brevva keys in that list and turn on Allow Full Access, for the mic and for learning your words.

  4. Switch to it

    In any app, hold the globe key and choose brevva keys.
